## How attribution works here

- **`AUTHORS.md`** (this file) is the canonical list of people.
- **`pyproject.toml`** carries the authors in package metadata, so the names
  appear on the [PyPI project page](https://pypi.org/project/nexassure/).
- **`NOTICE`** carries the Apache-2.0 attribution notice, which anyone
  redistributing NexAssure is required to reproduce.
- **Git history** is the per-change record and is authoritative for who wrote
  what.
